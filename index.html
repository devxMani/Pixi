The loop

Every failure turns into a test, and every test gates the release.

01 test & bench

Scripted scenarios run three times each under clean, noisy and interrupted calls. Only pass³ counts.

02 monitor

Every real call is logged with latency. Unhandled questions are flagged.

03 secure

Red-team probes for prompt leaks, jailbreaks and PII. Critical holes block the release.

04 improve

Every failure gets a patch, or you teach the answer. Autopilot loops until the gate is green.

How the loop decides

One experiment is one small change to the agent’s policy, judged on measured results.

1 · DetectRun every scenario against the active version. A scenario passes only if all of its explicit assertions pass.
2 · DiagnoseName the failure mode and quote the evidence from the reply or the policy.
3 · ProposeWrite one reversible change, never a full prompt rewrite.
4 · TestRerun the failed scenario, then every scenario that passed before.
5 · DecideAccept only if the target is fixed, nothing regressed and no critical safety probe broke. Otherwise reject, or mark inconclusive when evidence is incomplete.
6 · Keep historyEvery candidate is saved. The previous version stays intact and you can roll back.
